# The Hero's Journey — Bobby's spine, 2026-10-06

Raw from the intake paste. This is the **story engine**, not a
reference list: every myth we tell in the style of a classic moves
through these beats.

---

## The beats as Bobby wrote them

**Ch 1. ORIGIN — the starting point.**
Normal person. A worker. "Sheeple." Material body. Yoga at the gym.
Perceptual awareness. (Matrix, Lord of the Rings.) Introduce the
character, the scene, the friends, the workplace.
*Job? Government employee?*

**Ch 2. DISTURBANCE.**
Emotional. Fight with his girlfriend; she leaves him for his best
friend. Depressed, angry, unsettled. Throws himself into his work, or
whatever.

**Ch 3. INFLUENCE.**
A close friend, a smart trusted co-worker, brings up an interesting
thing that happened to her.

**Ch 4. CRACKS START FORMING.**
(Truman Show.) Coincidences. Questioning strange things happening.

**Ch 5. BREAK.**
Psychotic. Mental episode. Unforeseen happening.

**Ch 6. CALL TO ADVENTURE — refused.**
Circles twice. Loses his job. Running out of money. Getting nervous.

**Ch 7. CALL TO ADVENTURE — forced acceptance.**
(Wanted, Angelina Jolie.)

---

## Reading it

The important structural choice: **the refusal and the forced
acceptance are separate chapters.** Most tellings compress them. Bobby
keeps them apart, which means the hesitation is a scene with its own
beats — circling, job loss, running out of money, nerves — rather than
a single beat of reluctance.

The protagonist starts as a **worker with a material body**, not a
chosen one. That matters for the far-medicine and far-law work: the
person who wakes up is someone who was asleep in a body and a job, and
the body is part of what has to be re-learned.

## Travels (same paste)

Notes that read as setting material for the stories:

1. **Himalaya** — Buddhist. Boudanath, Bhaktapur Gompa. Sitting +
   singing. Terma.
2. **Pushpati Temple, India** — born in the dirt. Tea, brick, bag on
   the back. Yogi. Pip / Ragged Dick / Siddhartha.
3. **Arabia** — shop-boy or servant, beaten. Qur'an. A real Sufi.
   Gurdjieff as fog.
4. **Andes** — hired hand, not tourist. Shaman. Dust and a porch.
5. **Scotland** — loch house. Millie. Edinburgh café, books. Dreams of
   a queen and a ship.

**The recurring shape:** a servant or labourer, not a tourist. Born in
the dirt. Prayer or text present. A teacher who is real, or a fog for
one who is not. *Pip, Ragged Dick, Siddhartha* — the same protagonist
three times over.
