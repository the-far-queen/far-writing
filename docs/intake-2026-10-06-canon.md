Hero's Journey
Chapter 1. ORIGIN, STARTING POINT - Normal person, a worker, sheeple, material body, Yoga at the gym, perceptual awareness (Matrix, Lord of the Rings). Introduce the character, scene, friends, workplace. JOB? Govt employee?

Chapter 2. DISTURBANCE - EMOTIONAL, fight with his girlfriend, she leaves him for his best friend, depresssed, angry, unsettled, throws himself into his work, or whatever.

Chapter 3. INFLUENCE - A close friend, a smart, trusted co-worker brings up an ineteresting thing that happened to her

Chapter 4. CRACKS START FORMING (Truman Show), coincidences, questioning strange things happening.

Chapter 5. BREAK HAPPENS (psychotic, mental episode, unforeseen happening)

Chapter 6. CALL TO ADVENTURE, refused, circles twice, loses his job, running out of money, getting nervous.

Chapter 7. CALL TO ADVENTURE, FORCED acceptance. (Wanted, Angelina Jolie)
